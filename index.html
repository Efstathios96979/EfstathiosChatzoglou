About

I develop and validate Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics (SPH) models for turbulent, free-surface and hydraulic flows. My PhD centred on turbulence modelling in DualSPHysics, where I implemented the Dynamic Smagorinsky model and linked SPH filtering with Large Eddy Simulation.

I work in C++, Python and MATLAB on GPU and HPC platforms, and validate against analytical solutions, experiments and hydraulic engineering benchmarks.

Research

Turbulence modelling in SPH

A Dynamic Smagorinsky formulation computes the model constant from the resolved flow, removing case-by-case tuning. Implemented and tested in DualSPHysics.

Hydraulic structures

3D simulation of flow through sluice gates and over weirs, comparing turbulence models and boundary conditions, and reproducing hydraulic jumps against experimental data.

Confined microflows

3D channels with abrupt expansion and contraction, assessed against analytical solutions.

Vegetated and open-channel flows

Open-channel flow over vegetated beds. A manuscript is under review.

Data-driven flow analysis

Convolutional neural networks that reconstruct dense open-channel flow fields from sparse data.
